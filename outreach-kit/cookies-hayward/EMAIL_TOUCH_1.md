## Why this email works (notes for your own use)

- **Subject line names a specific strain on their menu.** Inbox skim survives because it's not generic. Cookies-brand staff care about Cookies-brand SKUs.
- **First sentence proves you did the homework.** "148 of the 222 strains" is a specific, verifiable number. It signals "this is not a mass send."
- **Single ask.** A 15-minute call. Not a meeting, not a demo, not a contract. Lowest possible friction.
- **The "send the kit" CTA at the bottom** lets a busy GM reply with two words and get the full kit asynchronously without committing to a call — a real out for someone who's interested but slammed.
- **No attachments on Touch 1.** Attachments tank deliverability and trigger Promotions filtering. The kit goes on Touch 2.
- **No mention of Berner, brand-house upside, or national rollout.** Save those for the call. Pitching corporate-scale on Touch 1 reads as overreach.
