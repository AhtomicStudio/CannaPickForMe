# Cookies Hayward — 5-min Demo & Voicemail Scripts

This is the cheat sheet for the live conversation. Memorize the flow, not the words. The demo path below is engineered (using the actual app's effect-mapping data in `src/data/effect-map.json`) to land on a Cookies-brand hero strain — most likely Girl Scout Cookies, Gelato, Wedding Cake, or Runtz, all of which they stock.

---

## 5-minute demo flow (screen-share or in-person on your phone)

**Setup:** Open cannapickforme.com in the browser, sign out (so you're seeing the un-personalized flow). Pre-clear your stash to start fresh, OR pre-stage your stash with their hero strains visible. **Don't** demo from a developer console or admin view — show what their customer sees.

### Minute 0:00 — frame the conversation (30 seconds)

> "I'm going to walk you through what your customer sees, then show you exactly where Cookies Hayward's card would render. This will take five minutes."

Don't ask permission. Don't pre-amble. Get into the product.

### Minute 0:30 — age gate (10 seconds)

Tap "Yeah, Let's Go." Brief acknowledgment:

> "Standard 21+ age gate. Affirmation per device. We don't show any cannabis content before this."

Move on.

### Minute 0:40 — disclaimer screen (5 seconds)

Tap to dismiss. Note in passing:

> "Legal-jurisdiction reminder. Same one your compliance team would write."

### Minute 0:45 — Stash setup (45 seconds)

Tap **Select Strains**, then add 3–4 strains they stock. Recommended quick adds:

1. Girl Scout Cookies
2. Gelato
3. Runtz
4. Wedding Cake

> "These are four of the 148 strains in your menu that are also in our catalog. In a real session, the user would have already curated their stash — they're picking from what they actually own."

Tap **Done**. You're back at home.

### Minute 1:30 — Run the matcher (90 seconds)

Tap **Pick For Me**. Walk through the 4 questions out loud:

| Question | Tap this | Why this answer |
|---|---|---|
| Mood | **Romantic** | Heavily weighted toward Happy + Euphoric, hybrid type bonus |
| Goal | **Fun** | Happy + Giggly + Euphoric + Uplifted, hybrid bonus |
| Intensity | **Moderate** | Balanced — keeps the hybrid weighting clean |
| Vibe | **Datenight** | Happy + Euphoric + Giggly + Uplifted, strongest hybrid bonus (1.3×) |

> "Romantic mood, fun goal, moderate intensity, date-night vibe. The matcher is now scoring every strain in our user's stash against this effect profile. There's no LLM involved — it's deterministic. Same answers, same result, every time. You can audit it."

### Minute 3:00 — The result (90 seconds)

The matcher will land on Girl Scout Cookies, Gelato, Wedding Cake, or Runtz with a high match score (typically 80%+ on this path). When it does:

> "There's the moment. The user has just been told what to consume tonight. **This is where your card renders.**"

Point to the result screen. If they're in person, hand them the phone. Then:

> "On a Sponsored Strain partnership, when our matcher surfaces one of your three hero strains — like {{whichever it landed on}} — your branded card appears here, with your name, your hours, and a tap-to-directions link to 1004 B Street. The user has zero prompted intent to look elsewhere. They've just been told what to buy. We're telling them where."

Then tap **See strains beyond your stash** (Better Matches button) to show the catalog upside:

> "If the user's profile actually wants something they don't own, we suggest strains from the broader catalog. These suggestions are also Sponsored-Strain-eligible — same placement mechanic, same pricing."

### Minute 4:30 — Land the next step (30 seconds)

Close out:

> "That's the placement. Three questions for you: First — does the way the card renders match what your team would want to put in front of customers? Second — is $399 a month for the bundle in the right zone for a charter rate? Third — who else needs to be in the room for the next conversation?"

Three open questions. Stop talking. Listen.

## Demo recovery — if the path doesn't land on a Cookies-brand strain

About 1 in 8 runs lands on a non-Cookies strain (Permanent Marker, Horchata, Apples and Bananas — all of which they ALSO stock, but the brand-tie pitch is weaker). If that happens:

> "Look at that — Apples and Bananas, which is also on your menu. Different effect signature, but the placement mechanic is the same. Want me to run it again with a different mood profile to show you the Cookies-lineage path?"

Then re-run with mood: **chill**, goal: **fun**, intensity: **moderate**, vibe: **friends** — that path biases harder toward Cookies-brand hybrids.

**Do not** apologize for the result. The matcher working independently of your sales pitch is part of the credibility — it's not rigged.

## In-person walk-in script (60-second pitch at the counter)

**When:** Tuesday or Wednesday, 2–4 PM Pacific (slowest hours, GM more likely on the floor).
**What to bring:** Printed `CookiesHayward_Pitch_Addendum.pdf` (3 pages) and the standard `CannaPickForMe_Dispensary_Partnership_Kit.pdf` (7 pages), both in a clean folder. Your phone with the demo loaded.

Walk in. Don't shop. Approach the budtender or hostess at the front and say:

> "Hi — I'm Adam, I'm not a customer today. I'd like to leave some materials for your general manager about a partnership. Is the GM in, or is there a buyer I could leave this with directly? I'll be 60 seconds either way."

If the GM is in:

> "Quick version: built a free strain-matcher app, your shop's already in our catalog, and 148 of the strains we recommend are on your menu. I'd like 15 minutes on the phone to walk you through where your store would render in the app. The full proposal is in here — page 1 has the headline number. Best email to follow up on this?"

Hand them the addendum. Get the email. Leave. **Do not pitch further on the floor.** Their job right now is helping customers.

If the GM is not in:

> "Totally fine. Could you make sure they get this folder, and could I write the GM's email on the front so I can send a short follow-up note tomorrow?"

Get the email. Write it down on the front of the folder so they see why you're asking. Leave. Send the fallback Touch 1 email by EOD.

## What to remember when you're nervous

- **You're not asking for a favor.** You have data they don't. They have inventory you've already validated. The conversation is symmetrical.
- **Don't over-explain the matcher engine.** Their question isn't "how does it work" — it's "will it move SKUs." Show the placement, not the algorithm.
- **The brand-house upside (70+ Cookies stores) is for them to raise, not you.** If they ask "could this scale to other Cookies stores?" — answer yes, calmly. Don't bring it up first.
- **End every call with three open questions and stop talking.** Silence after a question converts. Filling silence with more pitch does not.
