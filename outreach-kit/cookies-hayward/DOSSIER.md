## Brand-house upside (the real opportunity)

Cookies Enterprises operates **70+ retail stores in 20 markets across 6 countries**. ([Source](https://cookiesdispensary.com/locations/hayward/)) A working partnership at Cookies Hayward is, in practice, an audition for Cookies corporate.

**The conservative ladder:**

1. **Hayward only** — $299/mo (Sponsored Strain) or $399/mo (bundled with Result Partner). Your foot in the door.
2. **Bay Area Cookies cluster** — if Hayward delivers a working monthly report by month 3, ask the Hayward GM for an introduction to the regional manager covering Cookies' Bay Area stores. The same partnership scales to 5–10 locations.
3. **Cookies corporate / national rollout** — If the regional pilot works, the next conversation is with Cookies Enterprises corporate. At national scale, the conversation moves from per-store SaaS pricing to a master services agreement. Reasonable ceiling: $20K–$50K MRR.

**Don't sell this on the first call.** Mentioning national rollout in Touch 1 will get you flagged as a daydreamer. Land Hayward, prove the placement works, and let the GM raise the question of expansion themselves on the month-3 review call.

## Channel strategy (in priority order)

1. **In-person walk-in** — Tuesday or Wednesday, 2–4 PM Pacific (slowest hours, GM more likely to be on the floor). Bring a printed Cookies-Hayward-specific addendum and the standard kit. Ask for the GM by name (after the phone call from step 2). 60-second pitch, leave the materials, ask for an email follow-up. This is the highest-confidence channel for an account this important.

2. **Phone first** to identify the right contact (script above). Don't pitch on this call; just get the name and email.

3. **Email** to the verified GM/buyer. Use the personalized Touch 1 in `EMAIL_TOUCH_1.md`. Subject line references one of the hero strains so it survives the inbox skim.

4. **Backup email** to `info@cookieshayward.com` (if it exists) or via the website contact form, asking for routing to the buyer.

**What NOT to do:**
- Don't fire off an email to `roy.gaviria@cookieshayward.com` based solely on directory listings. If Roy moved on, the email will either bounce or land with someone who's no longer the right contact — both are wasted attempts.
- Don't pitch Berner or Cookies corporate directly. Wrong altitude for a charter-stage product, and any cold pitch will route through gatekeepers that block early-stage vendors. Earn the introduction through the Hayward store.
