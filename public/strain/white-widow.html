White Widow

Hybrid · Brazilian Sativa × South Indian Indica

A balanced hybrid first bred in the Netherlands. Known for its white crystal resin buds and burst of energy.

About White Widow

Its lineage traces back to Brazilian Sativa × South Indian Indica. The terpene profile leads with myrcene, backed by caryophyllene and pinene, which brings an earthy, musky depth to the nose. The experience is commonly described as happy, euphoric, and uplifted, though everyone reacts a little differently. That mix earns it a spot in daytime rotations, when there is still stuff to get done.

White Widow FAQ

Is White Widow an indica or a sativa?

White Widow is a hybrid. It crosses Brazilian Sativa × South Indian Indica. Hybrids sit between indica and sativa, so the character comes down to the specific cut.

What does White Widow feel like?

Users most often describe White Widow as happy, euphoric, uplifted, energetic. Effects vary person to person, so start low and go slow, especially with a strain you have not tried before.

What does White Widow taste like?

Expect earthy, woody, pungent notes. The flavor tracks with its terpene profile: myrcene, caryophyllene, pinene.

Is White Widow better for daytime or nighttime?

White Widow leans daytime. Its reported effects pair better with getting things done than with melting into the couch.
