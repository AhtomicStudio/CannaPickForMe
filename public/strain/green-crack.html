About Green Crack

Green Crack comes from Skunk #1 × Unknown Indica genetics. The terpene profile leads with myrcene, backed by caryophyllene and pinene, which brings an earthy, musky depth to the nose. The experience is commonly described as energetic, happy, and uplifted, though everyone reacts a little differently. That mix earns it a spot in daytime rotations, when there is still stuff to get done.

Green Crack FAQ

Is Green Crack an indica or a sativa?

Green Crack is a sativa. It crosses Skunk #1 × Unknown Indica. Sativas are usually associated with upbeat, head-forward sessions.

What does Green Crack feel like?

Users most often describe Green Crack as energetic, happy, uplifted, euphoric. Effects vary person to person, so start low and go slow, especially with a strain you have not tried before.

What does Green Crack taste like?

Expect citrus, sweet, mango notes. The flavor tracks with its terpene profile: myrcene, caryophyllene, pinene.

Is Green Crack better for daytime or nighttime?

Green Crack leans daytime. Its reported effects pair better with getting things done than with melting into the couch.
