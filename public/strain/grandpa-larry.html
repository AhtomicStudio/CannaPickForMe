Grandpa Larry FAQ

Is Grandpa Larry an indica or a sativa?

Grandpa Larry is a indica. It crosses Larry OG × Granddaddy Purple. Indicas are usually associated with mellow, body-forward evenings.

What does Grandpa Larry feel like?

Users most often describe Grandpa Larry as relaxed, sleepy, happy, euphoric. Effects vary person to person, so start low and go slow, especially with a strain you have not tried before.

What does Grandpa Larry taste like?

Expect earthy, pine, woody notes.

Is Grandpa Larry better for daytime or nighttime?

Grandpa Larry leans nighttime. People most often reach for it to wind down once the day is done.
