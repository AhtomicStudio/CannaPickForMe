Purple Haze

Sativa · Purple Thai × Haze

A classic sativa that delivers dreamy, blissful euphoria and creative stimulation. Inspired by Jimi Hendrix.

About Purple Haze

Genetics-wise, Purple Haze is a cross of Purple Thai × Haze. Myrcene is the dominant terpene here, backed by caryophyllene and limonene, lending an earthy, musky depth. People most often describe the experience as happy, euphoric, and creative. That mix earns it a spot in daytime rotations, when there is still stuff to get done.

Purple Haze FAQ

Is Purple Haze an indica or a sativa?

Purple Haze is a sativa. It crosses Purple Thai × Haze. Sativas are usually associated with upbeat, head-forward sessions.

What does Purple Haze feel like?

Users most often describe Purple Haze as happy, euphoric, creative, energetic. Effects vary person to person, so start low and go slow, especially with a strain you have not tried before.

What does Purple Haze taste like?

Expect earthy, berry, sweet notes. The flavor tracks with its terpene profile: myrcene, caryophyllene, limonene.

Is Purple Haze better for daytime or nighttime?

Purple Haze leans daytime. Its reported effects pair better with getting things done than with melting into the couch.
