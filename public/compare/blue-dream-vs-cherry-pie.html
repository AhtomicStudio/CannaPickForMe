Blue Dream vs Cherry Pie

The Short Answer

Blue Dream and Cherry Pie are both hybrids, so this one comes down to the details. Reported effects overlap on relaxed, happy, and euphoric, which is why people cross-shop them. Where they split: Blue Dream is more often described as creative, while Cherry Pie leans giggly. Both lead with myrcene, so the aroma family is similar. Neither is objectively better. Match the pick to the night you are planning, and start low if either is new to you.
