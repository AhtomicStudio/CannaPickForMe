Do-Si-Dos vs Ice Cream Cake

The Short Answer

Do-Si-Dos and Ice Cream Cake are both indicas, so this one comes down to the details. Reported effects overlap on relaxed, happy, and euphoric, which is why people cross-shop them. On the nose, Do-Si-Dos leads with limonene and Ice Cream Cake with caryophyllene, so they smell and taste like different rooms. Neither is objectively better. Match the pick to the night you are planning, and start low if either is new to you.
